# ScoreSaber In-Song Leaderboard

<img width="726" height="382" alt="Screenshot 2026-11-07 012825" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/4d4cb8e3-d3d2-4f46-9b57-7945ba5b4a0d" />

ScoreSaber In-Song Leaderboard is a Beat Saber mod that brings ScoreSaber leaderboard information directly into gameplay. It allows you to see player rankings, scores, accuracy, and other information while playing a song without having to leave the game.

The mod also tracks your current performance and shows where your score would place you on the leaderboard as you earn more points.

## Features

- **In-Song Leaderboard:** View ScoreSaber leaderboard rankings while playing instead of having to check them separately.
- **Player Information:** Displays player names, rankings, submitted scores, and accuracy.
- **Live Score Tracking:** Tracks your current score during a song and updates your projected leaderboard position as you earn more points.
- **Full Leaderboard:** Displays leaderboard entries beyond the original top 10 limit.
- **PP Points:** Shows Performance Points (PP) for ranked maps.
- **Song Modifiers:** Displays the modifiers associated with submitted leaderboard scores when the information is available.
- **Player Pictures:** Displays player profile pictures alongside leaderboard entries.
- **Colored Information:** Uses different colors to make rankings, scores, accuracy, PP, and other information easier to distinguish.
- **Solo and Multiplayer Support:** Displays the leaderboard during both Solo and Multiplayer gameplay.

## How It Works

Once you start a song, the mod identifies the selected map and difficulty and retrieves its corresponding ScoreSaber leaderboard.

The leaderboard then displays the available player information and rankings. While you play, it tracks your current score and compares your points against the existing leaderboard scores to estimate where you would currently place.

As your score increases, your projected position updates automatically.

## Installation

1. Make sure you have a compatible Beat Saber mod loader installed.
2. Place the mod DLL into your Beat Saber `Plugins` folder.
3. Launch Beat Saber.
4. Start a Solo or Multiplayer song and the leaderboard should appear automatically.
